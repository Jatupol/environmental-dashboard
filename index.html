01Methodology & Frameworks

Built on internationally recognized standards. Every emission factor is documented and traceable — designed to be defensible under third-party verification, not just visually polished.

📐

GHG Protocol Corporate Standard

Scope 1 (direct), Scope 2 (electricity — both location and market based), and Scope 3 (value chain) inventory structure following the WRI/WBCSD Corporate Standard and Scope 2 Guidance.

📊

ISO 14064-1:2018

Boundary setting via operational control approach, with audit trail design supporting ISO 14064-3 third-party verification readiness.

🇹🇭

TGO Methodology

Carbon Footprint for Organization (CFO) calculation framework with Thailand grid emission factor of 0.4999 kgCO₂eq/kWh from TGO 2023 reference.

🌍

GRI 305 / CDP / SBTi

Reporting structure aligned with Global Reporting Initiative emissions standard, CDP Climate questionnaire, and Science-Based Targets initiative methodology.

📦

Ecoinvent 3.9 + IPCC AR5

Packaging materials (PET, glass, aluminum) from Ecoinvent database; refrigerant emissions using IPCC AR5 GWP100 values.

🎯

2030 Reduction Target

25% absolute reduction vs 2015 baseline aligned with Coca-Cola System SBTi commitment, with progress-to-target tracking and gap analysis.
